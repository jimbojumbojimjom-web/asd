Stormfall

Last player standing wins as the storm closes in. Everyone on this server shares one match. Grab crates for ammo, wood and health, and build walls, ramps and floors to climb and take cover.

WASD move, Space jump, Shift sprint, mouse look, click to fire or build. Keys 1–4 switch rifle, wall, ramp, floor (10 wood each). If the mouse won’t lock, drag with the right button or use the arrow keys to look.
